## Part B — Actuator-disk assumptions and mass conservation

**B1.** Briefly describe the streamtube control volume, the assumptions of the model, and why a fixed-area upstream cylinder is inappropriate.

**A stream tube is a flow boundary formed by streamlines through which no mass crosses. It is not a constant-area cylinder because as air accelerates downward from rest toward the far wake, conservation of mass forces the cross-sectional area to contract continuously as velocity increases.**
