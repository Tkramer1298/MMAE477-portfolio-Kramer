Sizing Table Discoveries: I found that the prices of extremely small components are high.
I believe this is due to everything within each component needing to be very precise.
Working with extremely small components causes extra time to produce and therefore causes pricing to rise.
I was also surprised with how much power could be ran through these tiny parts.
Even with small and sometimes fragile components, a small drone can combine to create mass amounts of power in order to move quickly and take sharp turns.
I believe as the semester progresses, I will be able to compile parts for a drone that will be cost efficient and still function at an extremely high level.
